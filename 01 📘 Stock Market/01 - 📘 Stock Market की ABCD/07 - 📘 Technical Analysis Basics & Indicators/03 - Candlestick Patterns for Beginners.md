# 🕯️ CANDLESTICK PATTERNS – Market Ki Asli Power 📊🕯️🔥

Sirf ek candle kafi nahi hoti, jab 2-3 candles milkar ek khaas pattern banati hain, toh market ki direction change ho jati hai. 

---

## 1️⃣ 📘 Concept – Pattern Kya Hai?

Candlestick patterns humein batate hain ki **Smart Money** (Bade Institutions) kya kar rahe hain. 

| Pattern Type | Meaning | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reversal** | Trend badalne wala hai. | Stop Loss tight karo / Entry lo. 🔄 |
| **Continuation**| Trend jari rahega. | Position hold karo. ⬆️ |
| **Neutral** | Market confuse hai. | Wait and Watch. 😴 |

---

## 2️⃣ 🧠 Kahani – "Thaka Hua Pehalwan" (The Tired Wrestler) 🤼‍♂️

Socho ek pehalwan (Buyers) price ko bahut upar le gaya. Lekin ab wo thak chuka hai. 
- Tabhi ek naya taaza pehalwan (Sellers) aata hai aur usey patakh deta hai. 
- Yahi hai **Bearish Engulfing!**

---

## 🚀 Top 3 "Money Maker" Patterns

### 1. Bullish Engulfing 🟢 (Buyers Ka Kabza)
Jab ek badi Green candle pichli Red candle ko poora nigal (Engulf) leti hai.
- **Meaning:** Sellers khatam, Buyers ne kamaan sambhaal li. 💪

### 2. Hammer 🔨 (Niche Se Rejection)
Choti body aur neeche lambi wick.
- **Meaning:** Price niche gira, par Buyers usey spring ki tarah upar khench laye. 📉📈

### 3. Shooting Star ☄️ (Upar Se Rejection)
Choti body aur upar lambi wick. 
- **Meaning:** Profit booking start ho gayi hai, market niche jayega. 📉

---

# 🎯 3️⃣ Scalping Strategy (Pattern Focus)

1. **Step 1:** Support ke paas Hammer ya Bullish Engulfing dekho. 🔨🟢
2. **Step 2:** Next candle ke open par buy karo.
3. **Step 3:** SL pichli candle ki wick ke niche.
4. **Step 4:** Target 1:2 Risk/Reward.

---

# ⚠️ 4️⃣ Common Mistakes (Important)

| Mistake | Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Kahin bhi trade lena** | Pattern sirf S/R ke paas hi kaam karte hain. |
| **Confirmation ka wait na karna** | Candle close hone do, tabhi entry lo. |
| **Context bhul jana** | Overall trend ke khilaaf mat jao. |
