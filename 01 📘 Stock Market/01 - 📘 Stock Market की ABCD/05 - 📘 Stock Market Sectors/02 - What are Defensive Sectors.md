# 🦷 FMCG & 💊 PHARMA – Manda Ka Ilaj 🛡️🏠🏥

FMCG (Fast Moving Consumer Goods) aur Pharma wo sectors hain jo market girne par bhi aapka sahara bante hain. Inhe **Defensive Sectors** kaha jata hai.

---

## 1️⃣ 📘 Pro Definition Table

| Sector | Top Products | Why Defensive? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FMCG** | Sabun, Tel, Biscuit, Maggi. | Log nahana aur khana nahi chhorenge. 🧼🍲 |
| **Pharma** | Dawaiyan, Vaccine, Healthcare. | Bimari market dekh kar nahi aati. 🏥💉 |
| **IT** | Software, Cloud, AI. | Duniya digital ho gayi hai. 💻🚀 |

---

## 2️⃣ 🧠 Kahani – "Baarish aur Chhata" ☔🛡️

Socho market mein bahut tez baarish (Recession/Mandi) ho rahi hai. 
1. Luxury cars aur Naye gharon ki bikri ruk jayegi. 📉🚗
2. Lekin kya log bimar hona band karenge? Nahi.
3. Kya log subah brush karna chhor denge? Nahi. 🪥

Isliye in companies ke shares bahut nahi girte. Inhe **Safe Heaven** kaha jata hai.

# 💰 3️⃣ Investment Strategy – "Dhire Chalo, Dur Chalo" 🐢🏁

- **Dividend Income:** Defensive stocks (visheshkar ITC, HUL) bahut accha dividend dete hain. 🤑
- **Stability:** Agar aapka portfolio bahut zyada volatile (upar-niche) hai, toh 20-30% paisa in defensive sectors mein daal dein.
- **सत्य का ज्ञान:** "Jab market mein darr ho, tab defensive stocks mein ghar ho!" 🛡️🏠✨

---

# ⚠️ 4️⃣ Common Mistakes

| Warning | Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Slow Growth** | Inse umeed mat rakho ki ye 1 saal mein paisa double karenge. 🐢 |
| **Valuation** | Kabhi-kabhi log darr ke maare inhe bohot mehnga khareed lete hain. |
| **Regulatory Risk** | Pharma mein USFDA ke rules ka dhyan rakhna padta hai. |
