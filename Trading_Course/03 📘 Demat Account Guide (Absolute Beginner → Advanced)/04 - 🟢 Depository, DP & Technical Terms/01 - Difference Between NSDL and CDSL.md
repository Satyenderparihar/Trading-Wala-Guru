# 🏦 NSDL vs CDSL क्या है?

## 📌 Definition (Simple)

👉 **National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL)**
👉 **Central Depository Services Limited (CDSL)**

👉 दोनों **Depository** हैं
👉 मतलब: जहाँ तेरे shares digital form में रखे जाते हैं 💰

---

# 🧠 Kahani (Real समझ)

मान ले तेरे पास gold है 💰

* पहले लोग घर में रखते थे (risk 😱)
* अब bank locker में रखते हैं 🔐

👉 उसी तरह:
👉 Shares = NSDL / CDSL में safe रहते हैं

# 📊 NSDL vs CDSL (Clear Comparison)

| Feature         | NSDL                                   | CDSL                                |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Full Name       | National Securities Depository Limited | Central Depository Services Limited |
| Launch          | 1996                                   | 1999                                |
| Linked Exchange | National Stock Exchange                | Bombay Stock Exchange               |
| Account ID      | Starts with IN                         | Numeric                             |
| Popularity      | Institutions ज्यादा                    | Retail ज्यादा                       |
| Speed           | Fast                                   | Slightly simple                     |
