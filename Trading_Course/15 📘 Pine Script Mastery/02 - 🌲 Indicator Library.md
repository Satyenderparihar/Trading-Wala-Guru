## 🚀 How to Use
1. Open the [Clustering_Clouds_v2.pine](./indicater pinescripts/Clustering_Clouds_v2.pine) file.
2. Copy the entire code.
3. Paste it in TradingView **Pine Editor**.
4. Click **Add to Chart**.
