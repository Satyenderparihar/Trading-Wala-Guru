# What is Forex Trading

# 💱 What is Forex Trading? – Simple, Clear & Powerful Guide 🚀

Forex Trading ka matlab hai **Currency ko buy aur sell karna** profit ke liye 💰

Forex = Foreign Exchange

---

# 1️⃣ 📘 Definition – Forex Kya Hai?

Forex market me hum ek currency ko dusri currency ke against trade karte hain.

Example:
EURUSD

Iska matlab:

* EUR = Euro
* USD = US Dollar

Agar aapko lagta hai Euro strong hoga → Buy
Agar lagta hai Dollar strong hoga → Sell

---

# 2️⃣ 🧠 Kahani – Real Life Example

Socho aap Dubai gaye 🌍

India me ₹1 = 1 unit
Dubai me exchange karte ho → Dirham milta hai

Agar baad me rate change ho jaye aur aapko zyada ₹ mil jaye,
toh aapko profit ho gaya 😎

Yahi Forex trading hai – bas scale bada hota hai.

---

# 3️⃣ Forex Kaise Work Karta Hai?

Currencies pair me trade hoti hain.

| Pair   | Meaning         |
| ------ | --------------- |
| EURUSD | Euro vs Dollar  |
| GBPUSD | Pound vs Dollar |
| USDJPY | Dollar vs Yen   |
| XAUUSD | Gold vs Dollar  |

# 4️⃣ Forex Se Paisa Kaise Banta Hai?

Example:

EURUSD price = 1.1000
Aapne Buy kiya

Price gaya 1.1020

Difference = 20 Pips 💹

Profit = Pips × Lot Size

---

# 5️⃣ Forex Market Kab Khulta Hai?

Forex market 24 hours chalta hai (Monday–Friday)

Sessions:

| Session  | Time      |
| -------- | --------- |
| Asian    | Morning   |
| London   | Afternoon |
| New York | Evening   |

Most movement → London + New York overlap 🔥

---

# 6️⃣ Forex vs Stock

| Forex          | Stock         |
| -------------- | ------------- |
| Currency trade | Company share |
| 24 hr          | Limited hours |
| High liquidity | Moderate      |
| Leverage high  | Lower         |

---

# 7️⃣ Forex Me Risk Kya Hai?

✔ Leverage
✔ Overtrading
✔ News spikes
✔ Emotional trading

Isliye rule:

👉 1% Risk per trade
👉 Stop Loss mandatory
👉 Structure + Candle confirmation
