# 📊 TRENDMETER – Market Ki "Raftar" Ka Speedometer 🏎️🔥

Satyender bhai, yeh indicator chart par ek **Visual Speedometer** bana deta hai. Yeh aapko batata hai ki market kitni tezi se 'Buy' ya 'Sell' zone mein ja raha hai.

---

## 1️⃣ 📘 Concept – Speedometer Kyun?

Harr koi RSI numbers padhta hai (Jaise 70, 30), par **TRENDMETER** usey ek gaadi ke speedometer ki tarah dikhata hai. 

| Meter Zone | Meaning | Psychological Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Buy (Lime)** | Strong Upward momentum. | Riding the wave. 🚀 |
| **Sell (Red)** | Strong Downward momentum. | Extreme Caution / Shorting. 📉 |
| **Neutral (Gray)** | No clear direction. | Wait for the breakout. 😴 |
| **Retrace (Yellow)** | Trend thak chuka hai, wapas aa sakta hai. | Partial Profit Booking. 💰 |

---

## 2️⃣ 🧠 Logic – Backend Pe Kya Ho Raha Hai? 🤖

Yeh indicator **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** ka use karta hai:
- RSI 0 is left side.
- RSI 50 is middle (Neutral).
- RSI 100 is right side.

Jitna needle (Sui) right side hogi, utna zyada market 'Overbought' ya 'Strong Buy' zone mein hoga.

---

## 📊 3️⃣ How to Use – Pro Trader Tips

1.  **Lower Window:** Issey chart ke niche ek separate window mein lagaao.
2.  **Visual Confirmation:** Jab sui 'Buy' zone mein ho aur chart par Bullish Engulfing bane, toh High Probability trade hoti hai.
3.  **Divergence:** Agar chart upar ja raha ho par TRENDMETER ki sui niche girne lage, toh samajh lo trend khatam hone wala hai. ⚠️

---

# 💹 4️⃣ The "Speed" Strategy

- **Entry:** Jab needle 'Neutral' se 'Buy' ya 'Sell' zone mein move kare.
- **Exit:** Jab needle extreme 'Retrace' (Yellow) zone ko touch kare.
- **सत्य का ज्ञान:** "Speedometer yeh nahi batata ki kahan jana hai, yeh batata hai ki kitni tezi se jana hai!" 🏎️📉📈
