# 12.2 - Chart Pattern Cheat Sheet (Quick Reference)

Chart patterns market ke consolidation aur breakout phases ko identify karne mein help karte hain.

### 📐 Triangle Patterns
*   **Ascending Triangle**: Flat resistance aur rising support. (Bullish Bias)
*   **Descending Triangle**: Flat support aur falling resistance. (Bearish Bias)
*   **Symmetrical Triangle**: Support aur Resistance dono ek point ki taraf converge ho rahe hain. (Breakout jis taraf ho, wahi entry).

### 🚩 Flag & Pole Patterns
*   **Bull Flag**: Ek pole (uptrend) ke baad niche ki taraf halka channel. (Strong Bullish Continuation)
*   **Bear Flag**: Ek pole (downtrend) ke baad upar ki taraf halka channel. (Strong Bearish Continuation)

### 📈 Reversal Patterns
| Pattern Name | Trend | Implication |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Double Top (M)** | Uptrend | Do baar rejection ke baad market niche girta h. |
| **Double Bottom (W)** | Downtrend | Do baar support lene ke baad market upar jata h. |
| **Head & Shoulders** | Uptrend | Ek bada peak aur do chote peaks. Strong reversal. |
| **Inv. Head & Shoulders**| Downtrend | Head & Shoulders ka ulta. Bullish reversal. |

### ⛏️ Wedge Patterns
*   **Rising Wedge**: Price upar ja raha h par narrowing channel mein. (Bearish Reversal/Continuation)
*   **Falling Wedge**: Price niche ja raha h narrowing channel mein. (Bullish Reversal/Continuation)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Confirmation is Key**: Pattern banne ke baad hamesha "Neckline" ya "Trendline" ke breakout aur **Retest** ka wait karein.
