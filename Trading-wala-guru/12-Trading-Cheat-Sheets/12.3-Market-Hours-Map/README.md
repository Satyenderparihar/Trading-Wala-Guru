# 12.3 - Market Hours Map (Timing & Overlaps)

Global markets kab khulte hain aur kab band hote hain, ye janna bohot zaroori h kyunki **Volatility** overlap ke waqt sabse zyada hoti h.

### 🕒 Major Global Sessions (IST Time)
| Market Session | Opening Time | Closing Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🇦🇺 Sydney** | 04:30 AM | 01:30 PM |
| **🇯🇵 Tokyo** | 05:30 AM | 02:30 PM |
| **🇮🇳 Indian (NSE)** | 09:15 AM | 03:30 PM |
| **🇬🇧 London** | 01:30 PM | 10:30 PM |
| **🇺🇸 New York** | 06:30 PM | 03:30 AM |

### 🔥 The "Power Overlaps" (Best Time to Trade)
Jab do bade markets ek saath open hote hain, tab volume sabse high hota h:

1.  **London + New York (06:30 PM - 10:30 PM IST)**: 
    *   Subse zyada volatility. EUR/USD aur GBP/USD ke liye best time.
2.  **Tokyo + London (01:30 PM - 02:30 PM IST)**:
    *   JPY aur GBP pairs mein achi movement milti h.
3.  **Sydney + Tokyo (05:30 AM - 01:30 PM IST)**:
    *   Asian pairs aur AUD/NZD ke liye active window.

### 📊 Indian Market vs Foreign Market
*   **NSE (Equity)**: Sirf 09:15 AM to 03:30 PM.
*   **NSE (Commodity/Forex)**: Subah se raat tak chalta h, lekin shaam 6 PM ke baad (NY open ke waqt) movement badh jati h.

> [!NOTE]
> Markets 24/5 khule hote hain, par **Overlaps** mein trade karna hamesha profitable hota h kyunki spread kam aur liquidity zyada hoti h.
